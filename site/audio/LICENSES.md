`python -m tasks.whisper_silence audio --ids ...`). The Whisper transcripts quoted on the site are for the **whole**
clip (5–25 s), not only the excerpt.

Each ESC-50 recording is 5 s, so an 8 s excerpt holds material from the first one or two recordings listed in the
clip's manifest; both are attributed above. Per-clip attributions come from the ESC-50 `LICENSE` file. ESC-50 as a
whole is CC BY-NC 3.0; only ESC-10 classes (dog, rain, crackling fire, ...) were chosen so the excerpts are CC BY.
LibriSpeech speech is CC BY 4.0 and is exported only in the two speech-over-background clips above. **No FMA music is exported:** the music clips come from `lewtun/music_genres_small`, whose rows carry no FMA track id, so we cannot trace each track's licence. That is why the music card on the site has no player.
